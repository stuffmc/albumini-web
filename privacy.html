Privacy Policy

Albumini for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro

In short: Albumini does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything it does happens on your devices.

What Albumini reads

To find empty and duplicate albums, Albumini asks for access to your photo library. It reads the names of your albums and folders and counts how many photos and videos each album holds. It shows an album’s photos only when you ask it to. None of this leaves your device.

What Albumini changes

Albumini deletes an album or folder only when you ask it to, only when it is empty, and only after you confirm in the system’s dialog. It never deletes or changes your photos and videos. On the Mac, Albumini can ask the Photos app to show an album you chose.

Data kept on your devices

Albumini keeps the album list from your last scan and your settings on the device, so it opens quickly. For its widgets, its Top Shelf on Apple TV, and its Apple Watch app, it shares counts and a few album names with them on the same device, and sends them from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch. Deleting the app removes this data.

No tracking, analytics, or advertising

Albumini contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party code, does not track you, and does not connect to any server of ours.

Purchases

Albumini Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment or personal details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Albumini collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
